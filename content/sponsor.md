BSides Detroit is community-run and sponsor-supported. Sponsors help keep the event affordable, practical, and accessible while supporting the people doing security work across the region.

## Why sponsor

- Support the Detroit security community.
- Help keep ticket prices approachable.
- Reach practitioners, students, volunteers, and security leaders.
- Fund venue, badges, food, logistics, speaker support, and community programming.
