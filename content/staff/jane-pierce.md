Jane Pierce enjoys organizing conferences, a hobby she’s pursued since the 1970s. Jane began teaching in the Detroit Public Schools in 1991 and expanded to include adult education soon after. Her calling as an educator has facilitated practical development for thousands of students in venues ranging from large conventions to intimate classrooms. In addition to teaching, Jane currently owns her own company offering psychic services.
