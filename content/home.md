BSides Detroit is back on the wire.

After the restart, 2025 and 2026 are officially in the books. What began as getting the conference moving again has turned back into a rhythm: people showing up, sharing hard lessons, helping each other, and making the Detroit security community stronger.

The next signal is still forming. For now, this site is a holding pattern while we collect notes, thank the attendees, speakers, sponsors, volunteers, and organizers who made the last two years happen, and get ready for what comes next together.

BSides Detroit is about people first. Infosec matters, but there is no infosec without humans. The work is better when it helps the people and communities around us feel safer, learn more, and find each other.

That is the part worth preserving: real people, real lessons, real curiosity, and real action. We are carrying forward the conversations in hallways, the first talks, the late-night ideas, the volunteer hours, and the small moments where someone realizes they belong here.

BSides Detroit. Hack more. Help people. Secure all.
