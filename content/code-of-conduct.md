## Purpose

The BSides Detroit community is welcoming and inclusive to a vast number of contributors with varied and diverse backgrounds. As such, we are committed to providing a friendly, safe, and welcoming environment for all. And by all, we mean, including but not limited to, gender, gender identity and expression, sexual orientation, disability, physical appearance, body size, race, ethnicity, age, socioeconomic status, and religion (or lack thereof).

This code of conduct outlines our expectations for all those who participate in our community, as well as the consequences of unacceptable behavior. This code of conduct covers BSides Detroit meet-ups, BSides Detroit social platforms, and conferences hosted by BSides Detroit.

We invite all those who participate in BSides Detroit to help us create a safe and positive experience for everyone.

## Community Citizenship

A supplemental goal of this Code of Conduct is to increase community citizenship by encouraging participants to recognize and strengthen the relationships between our actions and their effects on our community.

Communities mirror the societies in which they exist, and positive action is essential to counteract the many forms of inequality and abuses of power that persist in society.

If you see someone making an extra effort to make sure our community is welcoming and friendly, and encourages all participants to contribute to the fullest extent, we would like to know.

## Expected Behavior

The following behaviors are expected and requested of all community members:

- Remember that BSides Detroit community event venues may be shared with members of the public; please be respectful to all patrons of these locations.
- Participate in an authentic and active way. In doing so, you contribute to the health and longevity of this community.
- Exercise consideration and respect in your speech and actions.
- Attempt collaboration before conflict.
- Refrain from demeaning, discriminatory, or harassing behavior and speech.
- Seek to learn from, rather than minimize or deny, the experiences of others.
- Be mindful of your surroundings and your fellow BSides Detroit participants. Alert BSides Detroit community leaders if you notice a dangerous situation, someone in distress, or violations of this Code of Conduct, even if they seem inconsequential.
- Remember that BSides Detroit community event venues may be shared with members of the public; please be respectful to all patrons of these locations.

## Unacceptable Behavior

We do not tolerate harassment of members in any form. Members violating these rules may be sanctioned or expelled from the BSides Detroit community at the discretion of the BSides Detroit leadership. The following behaviors are considered harassment and are unacceptable within our community:

- Violence, threats of violence, or violent language directed against another person.
- Sexist, racist, homophobic, transphobic, ableist, or otherwise discriminatory jokes and language.
- Posting or displaying sexually explicit material.
- Posting or threatening to post other people’s personally identifying information (“doxing”).
- Personal insults, including but not limited to insults related to gender, sexual orientation, race, religion, or disability.
- Spreading rumors or information designed to malign or damage another member’s personal or professional reputation.
- Video or photography without permission of all individuals involved is forbidden.
- Inappropriate physical contact. You should have someone’s consent before touching them.
- Unwelcome actions that make someone feel uncomfortable, including sexual advances, suggestive comments or jokes, or inappropriate physical contact.
- Deliberate intimidation, stalking, or following (online or in person).
- Sustained disruption of community events, including talks and presentations.
- Promoting or engaging in illegal activities.
- Advocating for or encouraging any of the above behavior.

Unacceptable behavior from any BSides Detroit community member, including speakers, sponsors, and those with decision-making authority, will not be tolerated. Anyone asked to stop unacceptable behavior is expected to comply immediately. If you do not wish to comply with a request to modify your behavior, you may leave the event and file a grievance with BSides Detroit leadership. See the section entitled “Addressing Unacceptable Behavior” for more.

## Political and Religious Displays Policy

To foster a respectful and inclusive environment for all participants, overt displays of political affiliation or ideological messaging are not permitted at BSides Detroit events. This includes, but is not limited to, items such as campaign apparel (e.g., MAGA hats), politically symbolic accessories (e.g., keffiyeh scarves when worn as a political statement), or signage promoting political movements regardless of affiliation.

BSides Detroit respects individual religious expression. Discreet and respectful religious garments and accessories — such as kippahs, tichels, hijabs, or similar items — are welcome and supported as part of our commitment to inclusion. This policy is intended to maintain a focus on community, learning, and shared experience while minimizing potential distractions or conflicts rooted in external ideologies.

## Weapons Policy

BSides Detroit community members are expected to comply with all state and local laws. Weapons include but are not limited to firearms, explosives (including fireworks), and large knives such as those used for hunting or display, as well as any other item used to cause injury or harm to others. Anyone seen in illegal possession of one of these items will be asked to leave immediately and will only be allowed to return without the weapon.

## Substance-Use Policy

BSides Detroit strictly prohibits the use, possession, sale, distribution, or manufacture of any illegal substances. Additionally, the use, possession, or distribution of any non-prescribed or misused substances that could impair judgment, alter behavior, or pose a risk to the health and safety of oneself or others is also prohibited. This includes but is not limited to, prescription drugs used without a valid prescription or in a manner not intended by a medical professional.

## Reporting Guidelines

If you are subject to or witness unacceptable behavior or have any other concerns, please notify BSides Detroit leadership as soon as possible. When reporting on unacceptable behavior, please add as much detail to the observed behavior by including a detailed description of the event, copies of the communication, and any other evidence that can be provided. BSides Detroit leadership will record every reported case of unacceptable behavior.

Additionally, BSides Detroit leadership is available to help community members engage with local law enforcement or help those experiencing unacceptable behavior feel safe, including but not limited to providing escorts, as desired by the person experiencing distress.

## Addressing Grievances

If you feel you have been falsely or unfairly accused of violating this Code of Conduct, you should notify BSides Detroit leadership at [info@bsidesdetroit.org](mailto:info@bsidesdetroit.org) with a concise grievance description.

The BSides Detroit community expects all members to immediately cease problematic behavior upon any reasonable request to do so. If you feel this request is unreasonable, you may leave the event and pursue the grievance process outlined above. Engaging further in problematic behavior after a request to stop will result in dismissing your grievance.

## Addressing Unacceptable Behavior

BSides Detroit leadership will investigate every reported unacceptable behavior and has the right to take immediate action they deem appropriate, up to and including a temporary ban or permanent expulsion from the community without warning and, in the case of social events, workshops, or conferences, without refund. In the case of minor offenses, BSides Detroit leadership has adopted a three-strike policy:

1. Issue a warning to those involved in the unacceptable behavior.
2. Issue a final warning and a reminder that continued unacceptable behavior will result in a permanent ban from all BSides Detroit community events and activities.
3. A permanent ban from all BSides Detroit community events and activities.

## Scope

We expect all BSides Detroit members (contributors, paid or otherwise; sponsors; and other guests) to abide by this Code of Conduct in all community events–online and in-person–and all one-on-one communications pertaining to the BSides Detroit community.

This code of conduct and its related procedures also applies to unacceptable behavior outside the scope of the BSides Detroit community when such behavior can adversely affect the safety and well-being of the BSides Detroit community and its members. BSides Detroit leadership reserves the right to disinvite or ban individuals from the community if they engage in harassing or discriminatory behavior at other community events. This includes any unacceptable behaviors listed in section 4 of this CoC.
