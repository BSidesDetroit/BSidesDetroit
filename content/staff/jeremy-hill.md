Jeremy Hill is a natural-born tinkerer and used to get in trouble as a kid for taking things apart. (Still happens sometimes.) Tech has been a significant part of his life ever since his grandfather taught him how to play games on a Commodore 64 and navigate DOS on the command line at the age of six. A veteran of the fire service, he spent 10 years serving his home community in Chattanooga, TN and now brings his love of helping others to the hacker community. Now a therapist in the making, Jeremy is a staunch advocate for neurodiversity and reshaping mental health stigmas. He's also a lover of music, cycling, philosophy, psychology, and meditation practice.
